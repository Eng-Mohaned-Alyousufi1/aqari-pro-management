# Aqari Pro – Real Estate Management & Property Marketplace Platform

**Aqari Pro** is a modern and comprehensive real estate platform designed to simplify the process of searching for, listing, managing, renting, and selling real estate properties. The platform aims to provide a reliable digital environment that connects property owners, property seekers, and system administrators through an organized and user-friendly system.

The platform addresses common problems in the traditional real estate market, such as difficulty finding suitable properties, limited access to accurate property information, inefficient communication between owners and customers, reliance on intermediaries, and the lack of centralized property management.

Aqari Pro allows property owners to create and manage property listings by providing detailed information such as property type, price, location, images, specifications, facilities, availability status, and other relevant attributes. Property seekers can browse available properties, search and filter listings according to their needs, view complete property details, save properties to their favorites, and contact the property owner or the platform administration.

The platform also includes an **administrative management system** that allows administrators to manage users, properties, categories, locations, property listings, reports, notifications, and other system operations. Administrators can review and control published properties, manage platform content, monitor user activity, and maintain the overall quality and reliability of the platform.

Aqari Pro is designed with a scalable architecture so that additional features and services can be integrated in the future. These may include advanced property search, map-based property discovery, notifications, featured property listings, advertising services, payment integration, analytics, reporting, and other smart real estate services.

## Main Users

The platform primarily serves three types of users:

1. **Property Seekers**

   * Search for available properties.
   * Browse property listings.
   * Filter properties based on specific requirements.
   * View detailed property information.
   * Save properties to favorites.
   * Contact property owners or administrators.

2. **Property Owners**

   * Create property listings.
   * Add property details, images, prices, and locations.
   * Edit and manage their properties.
   * Update property availability.
   * Monitor their published listings.
   * Communicate with potential customers.

3. **Administrators**

   * Manage users and accounts.
   * Manage property listings.
   * Manage categories and locations.
   * Review and approve property advertisements.
   * Manage featured properties.
   * Manage reports and notifications.
   * Monitor and control the overall platform.

## Core Objective

The primary objective of Aqari Pro is to transform traditional real estate services into an organized digital platform that makes property discovery and management easier, faster, and more accessible while reducing unnecessary intermediaries and improving communication between property owners and property seekers.

## Platform Vision

Aqari Pro aims to become a comprehensive digital real estate ecosystem that provides users with a trusted, organized, and efficient way to discover and manage real estate properties, while providing administrators with the tools required to operate and control the entire platform effectively.
